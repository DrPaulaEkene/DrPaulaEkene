# Paula Ekene Anugha

Data Science Analyst and Data Storyteller in UK public health.

I trained in pharmacy, then public health and data science. I did not switch to data. I continued. Today I turn open data from ONS, NHS and OHID into evidence people can act on, and I pair the numbers with real conversations, because a rate on its own never tells you who is behind it.

**Projects**

- [Alcohol-specific deaths, UK 2024](https://github.com/DrPaulaEkene/alcohol-specific-deaths-uk-2024) (coming soon): one ONS dataset analysed in Excel, Power BI, Tableau, SQL, SPSS and Python
- [Uterine fibroid ultrasound classification](https://github.com/DrPaulaEkene/Image-Classification-with-Keras): ResNet50 and MobileNetV3 on 2,200 ultrasound images
- [DataForge](https://github.com/DrPaulaEkene/DataForge): synthetic patient records for safe practice and testing
- [Renewables and urbanisation](https://github.com/DrPaulaEkene/The-Dynamics-of-Global-Environmental-Indicators-in-Climate-Change) : clustering and forecasting across 30 years of country data

**Tools:** Python, SQL, Power BI, Microsoft Fabric, Excel, Tableau, SPSS

*I find the human story inside the data.*
